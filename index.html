FAQ

Common questions from Orange County customers.

Who will do the work?

John Wright does every job himself. The plumber who gives you the estimate is the same plumber who does the work.

Is Wright Plumbing licensed and insured?

Yes. Wright Plumbing holds California State License #584239 and is bonded and insured. You can check any contractor's license on the Contractors State License Board (CSLB) website.

Are your estimates free?

Yes. I look at the job, explain what's needed and give you an upfront, flat-rate price before any work starts.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. I work with restaurants, retail stores, offices, multi-family buildings and industrial sites, including backflow certification, grease interceptors, tenant build-outs and preventive maintenance plans.

Which cities do you serve?

I serve Anaheim, Santa Ana, Orange, Garden Grove, Fullerton, Huntington Beach, Costa Mesa and surrounding Orange County cities. If your city isn't listed, give me a call.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. All work is done to local plumbing code, and I pull permits where they're required.
